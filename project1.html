About the Project

XplainCredit is an AI-powered loan default risk assessment system that predicts whether a loan applicant is likely to default and — more importantly — explains exactly why. Built for transparency in lending, it mirrors what real Indian NBFCs and fintech companies need for responsible credit decisions.

Key Features

  • 4-tier risk classification: Low / Medium / High / Very High
  • SHAP-based explainability — shows which factors drove the decision
  • What-If Simulator — change CIBIL score, income, or loan amount and see risk update live
  • Actionable improvement tips for loan applicants
  • Comparison of 3 ML models: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, and XGBoost

Tech Stack

Python, XGBoost, SHAP, Streamlit, Scikit-learn, Pandas, Matplotlib
