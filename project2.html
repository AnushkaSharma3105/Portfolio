Live Demo

About the Project

CloudQuote is a web-based pricing calculator built for the sales team at Tata TeleServices. It replaces manual Excel-based quoting by allowing users to instantly calculate accurate cloud infrastructure prices across multiple products, operating systems, and billing tiers — and export professional quotations in one click.

Key Features

  • Pricing across 185+ VM configurations for Vayu Cloud, Hana Grid, and OLVM
  • Supports 6 operating systems and 3 billing tiers (Hourly, 1-Year, 3-Year Reserved)
  • Add-on cost computation for storage, backup, firewall, and public IPs
  • Downloadable quotations in CSV and Excel with unique tracking IDs
  • 100% deterministic pricing — direct lookup from source Excel data, zero estimation

Tech Stack

Python, Streamlit, Pandas, OpenPyXL, XlsxWriter
